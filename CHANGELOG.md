## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Design-stage work from the overnight run of 2026-11-18: proposed ADRs 0003 to 0005, matching test suite, synthetic estate generator, local matching engine, Dataverse schema design, public roadmap collector, documentation skeleton.
- ADR 0006: languages for local tooling.
- Phase 1, signals live (accepted by Simon 2026-11-19):
  - **Collectors** in `ChangeRadarCore`, generated by `solutions/design/flows/New-CollectorFlows.ps1`:
    - Graph service health, every 15 minutes.
    - Graph Message Center, hourly.
    - Public roadmap, daily.
    - A daily horizon recompute.
    - Each run is logged in `crdr_collectionrun`, and every change is versioned in `crdr_signalversion`.
  - **Graph authentication:** the flows call Graph as `crdr-reader` with its Key Vault certificate, held in a secret environment variable (spike T6).
  - **Change Radar app:** a minimal model-driven app. The "Change Radar overview" dashboard shows signals by horizon (stacked by source) and by source, the Now and Imminent lists, and recent collection runs. Signals have Now, Imminent and Future views.
  - **Dev deploy scripts:** `setup/dev/Deploy-CollectorsToDev.ps1` and `setup/dev/Deploy-AppToDev.ps1`. Both plan by default and refuse any environment other than pp-lab.
  - **Queries and samples:** `queries/graph-service-health.ps1` and `queries/graph-message-center.ps1`, with redacted samples in `samples/graph/`, plus the reader token helper `queries/ReaderAuth.ps1`.
  - **Config rows:** `horizon_now_days`, `horizon_imminent_days`, `horizon_recent_days`, `horizon_roadmap_days` and `graph_services`.

### Decided

- ADR 0003 accepted: publisher Change Radar, prefix `crdr`; the schema design now uses it.
- ADR 0004 accepted: the LLM runs in-tenant only for now.
- Overnight assumptions Q2 to Q17 accepted, including the matching semantics; Q1 superseded by ADR 0006.
- ADR 0007 accepted and amended: signal horizons.
  - Message Center Imminent covers posts with an action date, plus plan-for-change posts published recently.
  - Roadmap items have their own 45-day window.
  - Slipped roadmap items (still in development after their GA month) stay in Future.
- ADR 0005: the phase 6 home view must filter in place and drill down by product, workload and impact. Phase 1's standard chart drill-down was not good enough.
